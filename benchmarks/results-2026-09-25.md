# Benchmark results — 2026-09-25

- Host: Raspberry Pi 5 16 GB, Debian 13.4 (aarch64, Linux-6.12.75+rpt-rpi-2712-aarch64-with-glibc2.41)
- Hailo firmware: 4.23.0 (release,app,extended context switch buffer)
- NPU: Hailo-8L (AI HAT+, 13 TOPS)
- Model: `models/yolov8n.hef`, input 640x640x3 uint8
- Frame source: synthetic random noise, seed 42 (no camera)
- 500 timed frames after 10 warmup; time.perf_counter() around HailoInference.run()
- FPS = 1000 / mean latency (sequential, batch 1)
- Command: `python benchmarks/run_benchmark.py --label <label> --out benchmarks/results-2026-09-25.md`

| Variant | FPS | mean ms | p50 ms | p95 ms | p99 ms | min ms | max ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: activate per frame (baseline) | 80.16 | 12.48 | 12.07 | 13.36 | 17.56 | 11.84 | 32.79 |
| B: activate once (2a) | 144.71 | 6.91 | 6.83 | 7.32 | 8.30 | 6.69 | 12.02 |
| A: activate per frame (repeat) | 80.99 | 12.35 | 12.06 | 13.10 | 15.47 | 11.81 | 18.95 |
| B: activate once (repeat) | 144.74 | 6.91 | 6.84 | 7.31 | 7.95 | 6.69 | 10.76 |

Variant B (network group activated and InferVStreams opened once, `run()` only calls
`infer()`) is what `src/hailo_inference.py` now does. Runs were ordered A, B, A, B in one
session with no other process on the NPU and the CPU ~90% idle (`vmstat`).

## Live USB webcam (`infer_picamera2.py`)

- Command: `python infer_picamera2.py --source usb --headless --duration 60`
- Camera: UVC "HD camera" at /dev/video0, 640x480 YUYV (advertises 30 fps)
- Scene: camera was facing a dark scene; frames were near-black, so detections/tracks
  were essentially empty. Numbers reflect pipeline throughput, not a busy scene.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| duration | 60.1 s |
| camera frames delivered | 898 (15.0 FPS) |
| frames inferred + tracked | 898 (15.0 FPS) |
| steady-state 5 s windows | camera=15.0, inference=15.0 |

The webcam delivered 15 FPS, not its advertised 30, so the pipeline is camera-bound;
the Hailo path alone measured 144.74 FPS above.
